# Knight Hacks 2026
## SPOT Operational Intelligence Challenge

### Challenge statement

Robotic inspection systems can produce mission statuses, action results,
readings, anomalies, and event logs across many locations and assets.

The difficulty is not simply collecting the data.

The challenge is turning that information into an operational experience
that helps a person understand:

- What happened?
- Where did it happen?
- Why does it matter?
- What requires attention?
- Is performance improving or degrading?
- What should an operator investigate next?

### Your task

Build a solution that makes the supplied synthetic SPOT data easier to
understand, explore, or act upon.

Your solution may include, but is not limited to:

- dashboards
- web applications
- mobile experiences
- AI assistants
- alerting systems
- anomaly triage
- replay tools
- APIs
- predictive models
- operator workflows
- spatial visualizations

### Minimum expectation

Your project should demonstrate more than simply displaying raw tables.

A strong solution should surface relationships or operational insights
that would otherwise require manual investigation.

### Data available

The challenge kit contains synthetic:

- missions
- actions
- sensor / inspection readings
- anomalies
- locations
- assets
- replay events

### Evaluation themes

Solutions may be evaluated on:

1. Operational usefulness
   - Does the solution help a user understand what matters?

2. Insight
   - Does the solution uncover meaningful relationships in the data?

3. Usability
   - Can a user understand and navigate the solution quickly?

4. Technical execution
   - Is the implementation thoughtful and functional?

5. Creativity
   - Does the team approach the problem in an interesting way?

6. Explainability
   - Can the team clearly explain what the solution found and why?

### Important

All supplied challenge data is synthetic and fictional.

Teams should not interpret fictional facility names, coordinates,
measurements, or events as real operational information.
